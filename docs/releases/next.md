- The replay bar appears with the pets' hover menu and hides after the pointer leaves for about 800 ms. Hovering or focusing the bar reveals the menu; holding its thumb keeps the bar visible while dragging outside it.

## Busyness on its face

- Daily busyness now selects one of five desk-mouth expressions instead of changing pet size. Pet size follows only the size settings. TA's expression comes from a live heartbeat and is not applied during replay.
- Re-export mouth placements with `art/aseprite/rig/mouth.py` whenever animation strips change.
